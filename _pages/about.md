Hello! This is Srivarshinee Sridhar, and I am currently a first-year grad student at the Machine Learning Department, <a href = "">Carnegie Mellon University</a>, pursuing a Masters in ML. Previously, I was a Research Intern at <a href = "">Microsoft Research</a> under <a href = "">Dr. Jayashree Mohan</a>, <a href = "">Dr. Alind Khare</a> and <a href = "">Dr. Srikant Bharadwaj</a> where I worked on systems implications of LLMs, specifcially in Inference. 

In my journey of training myself to ask interesting and important questions, I have explored AI Safety[<a href = "">CLINIC</a>], and Efficient Inference[<a href = ""><i>Sutradhara</i></a>]. Deeply motivated by the need for Safe AI, I am currently exploring efficient pretraining from a safety-first approach.

Outside of research, I am intrigued by music: whether it is learning a new instrument, singing, or weaving elements together to produce a song! I also occasionally enjoy dissecting films and analyzing cinematography.

To learn more about my background, feel free to check out my Resume. I am always eager to chat about research or anything else, so please feel free to reach out to me on LinkedIn!
